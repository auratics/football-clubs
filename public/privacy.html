Privacy & Cookies

Most Irritating Club is a just-for-fun voting site. No account is needed, and we do not ask for your name or email.

What we store when you vote

  • The club you voted for and the time of the vote.
  • A random ID kept in a cookie (vid), so that you can vote only once per league per week.
  • A one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, to stop people voting many times. We do not store your actual IP address.
  • Your country, worked out from your connection, for the "by country" rankings.

Advertising

This site shows ads from third parties such as Google. These partners may use cookies to show ads and to measure them. You can find out more and manage ad personalisation at Google's advertising policies and Ad settings.

Disclaimer

Rankings reflect anonymous visitor votes only. They are not statements of fact about any club, and the site is not connected to any club or league.
